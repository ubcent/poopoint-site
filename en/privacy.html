Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · October 1, 2026. Draft addition for version 1.1: October 7, 2026.

This policy describes the iPhone app poopoint, also named “Я здесь какал” in Russian. The developer is Dmitry Bondarchuk. Contact: dvbondarchuk@gmail.com.

Your private journal

Place names, dates and times, ratings, tags, notes, your visibility preference, rounded coordinates and available place information are stored locally in the app on your iPhone. The developer does not receive these entries. Version 1.0 has no accounts, app-owned journal server, CloudKit synchronization, advertising or analytics.

Both visibility options, “Only me” and “Maybe share later”, save the entry locally. The second option does not publish an entry or send it to anyone.

Postcards: the upcoming version 1.1

This section describes the planned behaviour of version 1.1, which is still being prepared. The published version 1.0 does not create postcards.

In version 1.1, a postcard will be created locally on your iPhone. You will preview the image and choose its fields before sharing. The city/country, when available, and rating are shown by default; the place name, date and note are optional. You can also hide the city/country. Coordinates and addresses are not included in the image.

The image is passed to an app or recipient you choose only after you use the standard share sheet. They process the image under their own policies. The developer does not automatically receive the postcard, and the app has no publishing service. A temporary file is used for this transfer; the journal stays local. The “Maybe share later” preference still does not send or publish anything by itself.

Location

The app requests location access after you tap the add-place or centring action. Your location is used to choose a point and show your position on the map; there is no background tracking. You can decline access and select a place by pressing and holding the map.

Coordinates are rounded to an approximately 80-metre grid before saving. The original location is not recorded in the journal. Rounding reduces precision but does not guarantee anonymity or hide a recognizable place.

Apple services

Apple Maps supplies the map through MapKit. For an optional address lookup, the app sends only the rounded point to Apple's geocoding service, without the place name, note, date, rating or tags. A house number from the result is not stored.

Apple may process technical request information according to its privacy policy. An entry is still saved if there is no connection or an address lookup fails; the address remains unknown.

Storage and deletion

Entries stay in the local journal until you delete them. Open an entry's details, tap the delete action and confirm. Deleting the app removes its local storage.

App data may be included in normal iPhone backups according to your Apple settings. Backups are managed separately in system settings; deleting a local entry does not automatically remove earlier backup copies.

You can change or revoke location access in iOS Settings → the app → Location. Manual place selection continues to work without permission.

Support requests

If you email dvbondarchuk@gmail.com, the developer receives your sender address and message contents to respond. Email is hosted by Gmail under Google's policies. Do not send personal entries or your location unless needed to resolve the issue. To request deletion of correspondence from the developer's mailbox, write to the same address.

This website

The support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages have no forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. When someone visits a GitHub Pages website, GitHub records and stores the visitor's IP address for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. GitHub may also process technical request information according to its privacy statement. Visiting this site does not give GitHub or the developer access to the journal on your iPhone.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated before a release adds synchronization, public places or other data processing. Questions about the current version can be sent to Dmitry Bondarchuk at dvbondarchuk@gmail.com.
