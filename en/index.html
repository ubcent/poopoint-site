Frequently asked questions

Can I add a stop without location access?

Yes. Press and hold the place you want on the map to open the new-entry form. To allow location access later, open iOS Settings → the app → Location.

Where are my entries stored?

In the app's local storage on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, public feed or CloudKit synchronization. Its data may be included in normal device backups according to your Apple settings.

Why is the marker approximate?

Coordinates are rounded to an approximately 80-metre grid before saving. This reduces the precision of your private journal. Rounding does not make an entry anonymous.

What does “Maybe share later” mean?

It is a local preference for the future. It does not publish an entry or send it to anyone. Both visibility settings keep the entry on your device.

Why couldn't the app find an address?

Apple's service looks up an address using the rounded point. If a connection is unavailable or the service fails, the entry is still saved. You can try the address lookup again from the entry's details later.

How do I delete an entry?

Open a visit from the map or history, tap the delete action and confirm. It disappears from your journal. Deleting the app also deletes its local storage; backups are managed separately in Apple settings.
